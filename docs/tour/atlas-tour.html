    Atlas in two minutes

    Atlas is a web app where employees ask questions about company documents and get answers with sources. It can also take actions for them, like opening a Jira ticket or posting in Slack, but only after a person approves. Every step is shown live and recorded.

    This tour uses Atlas to teach modern AI engineering: the patterns and tools behind most AI products today. Each chapter is a short mission with a real problem to solve.

    One spine, two paths

    Every feature hangs off one path a message travels. Each chapter zooms into one part of it.

    Around the spine: ingestion turns documents into searchable pieces before anyone asks; Langfuse records every step; evals score Atlas on fixed test questions; memory carries facts about you between chats.

    How a mission works

    About 12 short screens. Each one asks you to do something: guess, pick, tap or click. Essentials takes about 10 minutes and needs no code. An optional deep dive walks through the code blocks that matter. Every mission ends with a cheat sheet you can come back to. Your progress is saved in this browser.

# Accounts, Passwords and VPN

## Passwords

- Use the company password manager for every work password.
- Passwords must be at least **14 characters** long and unique to each account.
- Never share a password, not even with IT. IT will never ask for it.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication (2FA) is **required** on every account that supports it. Use the authenticator app or a hardware key; text-message codes are not allowed.

## VPN

Use the VPN whenever you access internal systems from outside the office. Install it from the IT service portal under *Software → VPN*.

If you can't connect, restart the VPN app first. If that doesn't help, open a ticket in the IT service portal with a screenshot of the error.

## Access requests

Request access to a new tool or system through the IT service portal. Your manager approves the request; IT sets it up within 2 working days.

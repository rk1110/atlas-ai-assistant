# Hardware Policy

## Your laptop

Every employee gets a company laptop on their first day: a 14-inch MacBook Pro for engineering and design roles, and a 14-inch MacBook Air for other roles. Windows laptops are available on request.

## Replacement cycle

Laptops are **replaced every 3 years**. IT contacts you a month before your laptop's replacement date.

## Requesting a new laptop or a repair

To request a replacement or report a broken device, **open a ticket in the IT service portal**. Choose *Hardware → Laptop* and describe the problem. IT replies within 1 working day.

If your laptop is lost or stolen, report it to IT **and** to Security right away (see the Incident Reporting Policy).

## Accessories

You can order one external monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset through the IT service portal. Other accessories can be bought with the home-office allowance.

## When you leave Acme

Return your laptop and accessories on your last day. IT sends you a prepaid shipping box if you work remotely.

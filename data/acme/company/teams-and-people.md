# Teams and People

Who leads each team at Acme Corp, who they report to, and which Slack channel reaches them. Policy owners are listed in each policy's header.

## Leadership

- **Maria Jansen** is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO). She works from the Rotterdam headquarters.
- **David Okafor** is the Chief Technology Officer (CTO) and reports to Maria Jansen.
- **Sofia Reyes** is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and reports to Maria Jansen.
- **Arjun Mehta** is the Chief People Officer and reports to Maria Jansen.

## Teams

### Engineering

Led by **Lars de Vries**, Head of Engineering, who reports to David Okafor. About 140 people across Rotterdam and Pune. Slack: #eng-help.

### Product

Led by **Hannah Brooks**, Head of Product, who reports to David Okafor. Based in Austin. Slack: #product.

### Design

Led by **Yuki Tanaka**, Head of Design, who reports to Hannah Brooks. Slack: #design.

### IT

Led by **Priya Nair**, IT Manager, who reports to David Okafor. The IT Team owns the Hardware Policy and the Accounts and Passwords Policy, and runs the IT service portal. Slack: #ask-it.

### Security

Led by **Tom Becker**, Security Lead, who reports to David Okafor. The Security Team owns the Incident Reporting Policy and handles phishing reports. Slack: #security-incidents.

### Finance

Led by **Elena Petrova**, Finance Manager, who reports to Sofia Reyes. The Finance Team owns the Expense Policy and the Travel Policy, and runs the expense tool. Late expense claims (after 30 days) need Elena's team's approval. Slack: #ask-finance.

### People (HR)

Led by **Grace Liu**, Head of People, who reports to Arjun Mehta. The People Team owns the Time Off Policy, the Parental Leave Policy, the Working Hours Policy, the Hybrid Work Policy, the Learning and Development Policy and the onboarding programme. Slack: #ask-people.

### Sales

Led by **Marco Rossi**, VP of Sales, who reports to Maria Jansen. Based in Austin. Slack: #sales.

### Customer Support

Led by **Fatima Al-Sayed**, Head of Customer Support, who reports to Marco Rossi. Support runs from Pune and Austin, so customers are covered around the clock. Slack: #support.

### Communications

Led by **Noah Williams**, Communications Lead, who reports to Arjun Mehta. Owns the company handbook pages, including About Acme Corp. Slack: #announcements.

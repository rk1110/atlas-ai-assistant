# About Acme Corp

Acme Corp is a fictional company used to test Atlas. It makes **project-planning software for construction companies**.

## Key facts

- Founded in **2014** in Rotterdam, the Netherlands.
- About **420 employees** in 3 offices: Rotterdam (headquarters), Austin and Pune.
- Customers in 18 countries.

## Our mission

Help builders finish projects on time, every time.

## Our values

1. **Customers first:** we build what helps our customers finish their projects.
2. **Own it:** if you see a problem, you are responsible for raising it.
3. **Keep it simple:** clear writing, simple tools, short meetings.
4. **Better together:** we share knowledge openly across teams.

## Teams

Engineering, Product, Design, Sales, Customer Support, People (HR), Finance, IT and Security.

# Parental Leave Policy

Acme Corp supports every new parent, whatever their role, location or how they became a parent.

## How much leave you get

All parents get **16 weeks of fully paid parental leave**. This applies equally to birth parents, non-birth parents, adoptive parents and parents through surrogacy. There is no minimum length of service: you are eligible from your first day at Acme.

You can take the leave at any time **within the first year after the birth or adoption**. Leave that is not taken within that year expires.

## Taking your leave

- You can take the 16 weeks in one block, or split it into up to **three blocks** of at least two weeks each.
- Tell your manager and the People Team at least **8 weeks before** you plan to start, where possible.
- Submit the dates in the HR portal under *Time off → Parental leave*.

## Pay and benefits during leave

You receive your full base salary for the whole 16 weeks. Health insurance, pension contributions and vacation accrual continue as normal while you are on leave.

## Returning to work

Parents returning from leave can work a **reduced schedule of 4 days a week at full pay for the first 4 weeks** back. Agree the schedule with your manager before you return.

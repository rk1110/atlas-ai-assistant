# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme Corp! This page tells you what happens in your first weeks.

## Before day one

- IT ships your laptop to your home address. It arrives at least 2 working days before your start date.
- You receive an invitation to the HR portal to sign your contract and add your bank details.

## Your first day

- Join the welcome call at 10:00 with the People Team.
- Set up your accounts by following the IT checklist sent to your personal email.
- Meet your **onboarding buddy**: a colleague from another team who answers your questions for your first month.

## Your first week

- Read the policies in the handbook, starting with the Working Hours Policy, the Hybrid Work Policy and the Time Off Policy.
- Complete the mandatory **security awareness training** in the learning platform within 5 working days.
- Have a 1:1 with your manager to agree your goals for the first 90 days.

## Your first 90 days

Your manager checks in with you after 30, 60 and 90 days. At 90 days you have a short review to confirm your probation period.

## Where to get help

- People questions: #ask-people on Slack.
- IT questions: the IT service portal.
- Anything else: your buddy or your manager.

# Learning and Development

We want everyone at Acme Corp to keep growing.

## Learning budget

Every employee has a learning budget of **$1,500 per year** for **courses, books and conferences**.

- Get **approval from your manager before you spend** the money. A short message in Slack with the course or event and its cost is enough.
- Pay for it yourself and claim it back through the expense tool, attaching your manager's approval.
- The budget resets on 1 January. Unused budget does not carry over.

Travel and hotel costs for an approved conference can come from the learning budget or, if the conference is part of your role, from your team's travel budget. See the Travel Policy.

## Learning time

Everyone can spend **4 hours a month** of working time on learning. Block it in your calendar so your team knows.

## Internal programmes

- **Mentoring:** sign up in the HR portal to be matched with a mentor from another team.
- **Lunch and learn:** every other Thursday, a colleague presents a topic over lunch.
- **Certifications:** if a certification is required for your role, Acme pays for it outside your learning budget.

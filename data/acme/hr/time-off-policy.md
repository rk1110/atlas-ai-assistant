# Time Off Policy

This policy covers vacation and sick leave. Parental leave has its own policy.

## Vacation

Every full-time employee, including new joiners, gets **22 paid vacation days per year**. This is **in addition to public holidays** in your country.

- Vacation days are **accrued monthly**: you earn 22 / 12 ≈ 1.83 days for each month you work. New employees start accruing on their first day.
- You can carry over **up to 5 unused days** to the next year. Any days above that expire on 31 December.
- Request vacation in the HR portal at least **2 weeks in advance** for anything longer than 3 days.

Part-time employees get vacation days in proportion to their contracted hours.

## Sick leave

You get **10 paid sick days per year**.

- If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day, and log the absence in the HR portal.
- A **doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days**. Upload it to the HR portal.
- Unused sick days do not carry over.

Longer illnesses are handled case by case with the People Team; your salary is protected while we work it out with you.

## Other leave

- **Bereavement:** up to 5 paid days for the loss of a close family member.
- **Moving house:** 1 paid day per year.
- **Volunteering:** 2 paid days per year for registered charity work.

# Working Hours Policy

## Standard working time

A full-time position at Acme Corp is **8 hours a day, 40 hours a week**, Monday to Friday.

Everyone is available during **core hours, from 10:00 to 16:00** local time. Outside core hours you can choose when to start and finish, as long as your team knows how to reach you.

## Breaks

You take a **1-hour lunch break** each day. The lunch break is **not counted as working time**, so a typical day looks like 9:00 to 18:00 or 8:30 to 17:30.

Short breaks during the day are encouraged and are counted as working time.

## Overtime

Overtime must be agreed with your manager **in advance**. Approved overtime is compensated with time off in lieu: one hour off for each hour worked, to be taken within 3 months.

## Part-time work

Part-time arrangements are possible for most roles. Your hours are agreed in your contract; core-hour availability then applies on your working days only.

## Recording time

Engineering and Support teams record their hours in the time-tracking tool each week. Other teams only record overtime and absences.

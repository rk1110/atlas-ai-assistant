# Hybrid Work Policy

Acme Corp is a **hybrid company**: we combine focused work from home with time together in the office.

## How it works

- You can work from home **up to 3 days a week**.
- **Tuesday and Thursday are in-office days for everyone.** We schedule team meetings, workshops and social events on these days.
- On the other days, choose home or office based on your work.

## Working from home

- Your home workspace must have a reliable internet connection and a quiet place for calls.
- Acme provides a one-time **home-office allowance of $500** for a chair, desk or monitor. Claim it through the expense tool.
- Company security rules apply at home: lock your screen, use the VPN, and never let others use your work laptop.

## Working from another country

Working from abroad is limited to **20 working days per year** and needs approval from your manager and the People Team in advance, for tax and legal reasons.

## Exceptions

Fully remote arrangements are possible for some roles, for example for medical reasons. Ask your manager and the People Team; exceptions are reviewed every 6 months.

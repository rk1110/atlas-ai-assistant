# Security Incident Reporting Policy

Fast reporting limits the damage. Nobody gets in trouble for reporting a mistake; hiding one is the problem.

## What counts as an incident

- You clicked a link or opened an attachment in a **suspicious or phishing email**.
- You entered your password on a page you no longer trust.
- Your laptop or phone is lost or stolen.
- You sent confidential data to the wrong person.
- You notice unusual activity on any account.

## What to do

1. **Report it to the Security team within 1 hour**, in the **#security-incidents** Slack channel or by email to **security@acme.example**.
2. **Don't delete the suspicious email.** Security needs it to investigate. Don't forward it to colleagues either.
3. If you entered your password, change it right away with the password manager.
4. Disconnect your laptop from the network if Security asks you to.

## What happens next

Security replies within 30 minutes during working hours. They may ask questions, check your laptop remotely, or reset your accounts. You'll get a short summary when the incident is closed.

## Reporting a phishing email you didn't click

Use the *Report phishing* button in your email client. That's all: no Slack message is needed.

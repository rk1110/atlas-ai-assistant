# Expense Policy

This policy explains what Acme Corp pays for and how to get your money back.

## General rule

Spend company money as if it were your own. Expenses must be for business purposes and supported by a receipt.

## Meals with clients

You can spend **up to $75 per person** on a client meal, such as a client dinner, without approval. **Anything above $75 per person needs your manager's approval before** the meal. Alcohol is included in the per-person limit.

Team meals and celebrations are paid from your team's budget and need approval from your manager in advance.

## Other common expenses

| Expense | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Home-office allowance | One-time $500, see the Hybrid Work Policy |
| Learning (courses, books, conferences) | From your $1,500 yearly learning budget, approved first |
| Mobile phone | Up to $40 per month if your role requires being reachable |
| Software and subscriptions | Request through the IT service portal, not as an expense |

## How to get reimbursed

1. **Submit the receipt in the expense tool within 30 days of the purchase.** Take a photo of paper receipts; digital receipts can be forwarded to expenses@acme.example.
2. Add the business reason and, where needed, your manager's approval.
3. Your manager approves the claim in the expense tool.
4. **Approved expenses are paid with the next monthly payroll.**

Claims submitted after 30 days are only paid with approval from Finance.

## What we don't pay for

Fines (for example parking tickets), personal entertainment, and upgrades to first or business class without approval.

# Travel Policy

## Before you travel

All business travel needs **approval from your manager** before you book. Book flights and hotels through the travel booking tool, so Finance can see the costs and we can reach you in an emergency.

## Flights and trains

- Book economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for longer flights.
- Choose the train for journeys under 4 hours where possible.
- Book at least 14 days in advance when you can: it is usually much cheaper.

## Hotels

Hotels must be booked through the travel tool. The nightly limit is $180 in most cities and $250 in high-cost cities (the list is in the tool).

## Meals while travelling

While travelling you get a **daily meal allowance of $60**. This covers your own meals only. Meals with clients follow the Expense Policy.

## After the trip

Submit any remaining costs, like taxis or meals, in the expense tool with receipts, following the Expense Policy.
